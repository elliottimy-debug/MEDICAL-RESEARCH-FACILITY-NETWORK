IT Security Policy Guidelines

The security policy sets the basic rules for protecting the facility's systems, network and information. The following guidelines apply to all employees, researchers, IT staff and contractors:

    Access Control and Authentication Standards

  • Employees are advised to use strong passwords that are at least 8 characters long and include a mix of upper/lower case letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • All employees are expected to change their passwords every 90 days. Sharing of passwords and writing them down is prohibited.
  • Multi-factor authentication is required for servers hosting the central research center management software and remote access to administrative systems.

    • Network Segmentation and Endpoint Security

  • The network must have logical separation (VLANs) of traffic for administrative, lab research, and guest users.
  • All computer equipment in the main block and lab machines must have updated antivirus, encryption, and host-based firewall protection. .

    • Data Encryption and Storage Protocols

  • All sensitive medical research data, patient information, and local backups must be stored using HTTPS encryption.
  • Storing confidential information on external storage devices such as hard disks and USB sticks is prohibited unless authorized by the IT department.
  • Important data must be backed up according to the facility's backup schedule.

    • Incident Response and Reporting

  • Any employee noticing unauthorized access attempts, malware infection, or physical security breaches must report it to the IT Help Desk immediately.

    • Physical and Environmental Security

  • Server rooms and networking closets must be secured at all times, and only authorized IT personnel should have physical access to them.
  • Uninterrupted power supply must be used for all networking and server equipment to avoid hardware or data loss due to power failures.
