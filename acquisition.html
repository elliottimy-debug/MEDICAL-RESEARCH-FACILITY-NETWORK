IT Acquisition Procedures and Approvals

To ensure proper financial management of the new research facility and to avoid purchasing hardware and software that do not meet the required standards, the following acquisition procedures must be followed:

    Needs Assessment

  • Heads of departments, administrators, and lab researchers who intend to acquire new computer hardware or software licenses must apply using the IT Requisition Form.
  • The form must indicate the intended use, number of users, projected costs, and whether the request is for replacement or enhancement.

    • Technical Review

  • IT Manager evaluates equipment specifications against existing hardware standards (e.g., Cat6 UTP requirements, 24-Port Managed Gigabit Switch compatibility).
  • The completed form may then be submitted to the facility managers and finance department for approval. It is at this point that an economic evaluation of the request will be undertaken.

    • Financial Sign-off

  • Procurements exceeding R 20,000 will require competitive quotations from approved vendors presented before final approval from Facility Management.

    • Licensing Procedures

  • Software acquisition must be licensed, and all digital assets must belong to the organization.
  • The IT department has the responsibility of ensuring that all software is properly licensed.

    • Asset Tagging & Deployment

  • Newly acquired hardware must be logged into the asset management system, configured according to IT security baselines, and deployed by IT technicians.
