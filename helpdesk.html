Service Desk / Help Desk Procedures

The service desk is the first point of contact for employees who experience IT problems. A ticket-based process gives the IT team a record of the problem and makes it easier to track work from the first report until the issue is resolved.

    Ticket Logging and Intake Channels

  • Users should log technical issues, hardware failure, and service issues via the IT Help Desk Portal, this can be done via email or phone-in logging
  • A unique ticket ID will be generated for every logged issue, capturing the description of the issue, location and timestamp

    • Priority and Severity Classification:

    Tickets are categorized and prioritized upon intake based on operational impact.

  • Critical (Priority 1):Network outages, server failures hosting research software, or major security incidents.
  • High (Priority 2):Workstation or printer failures impacting multiple users in a specific section.
  • Medium/Low (Priority 3 & 4):Software installation requests, account password resets, and routine hardware maintenance.

    • Service Level Agreements (SLAs) and Resolution Targets:

    The Service Desk has a strict service level agreement and resolution timeframe for logged tickets

  • Critical Issues:First response to ticket should be in less than 15 minutes, with an active resolution target in under 2 hours.
  • High priority issues:: First response should be in less than 1 hour with an active resolution target within 4 hours
  • Standard requests:A first response should be requested within 24 hours.

    • Incident Diagnostics & Resolution:

  • Technicians troubleshoot using standardized test protocols (e.g., ping tests, IP pool verification, VLAN assignment checks).

    • Ticket is closed upon user confirmation of issue resolution, followed by an automated customer satisfaction survey.
