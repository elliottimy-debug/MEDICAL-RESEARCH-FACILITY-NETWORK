Acceptable Use Policy

To ensure that administrative staff and lab researchers utilize the network and company hardware within the bounds of the law, company policies, and avoid potential legal issues, the following Acceptable Use Policy has been developed:

    System and Hardware Usage

  • Facility computer equipment, printers, and network systems are only for official organisational purposes and medical research activities.
  • Employees are responsible for the safety of their equipment and must ensure they are not left unattended without a password-protected screensaver.

    • Internet and Network Access Restrictions

  • Employees must not use the internet for personal reasons and are advised to use it only for official business, research, or administrative purposes.
  • Accessing or downloading explicit material is strictly prohibited.

    • Software Installation and Licensing

  • Employees are prohibited from downloading, installing, or running unauthorized software, free programs, or browser extensions on company computers.
  • Software installed on the organization's computer systems must be licensed, approved, managed, and monitored by the IT department.
  • Employees must not disable centrally managed antivirus, firewalls, or endpoint security software.

    • Software Installation and Licensing

  • Employees are prohibited from downloading, installing, or running unauthorized software, free programs, or browser extensions on company omputers.
  • Software installed on the organization's computer systems must be licensed, approved, managed, and monitored by the IT department.
  • Employees must not disable centrally managed antivirus, firewalls, or endpoint security software.

    • Email and Data Sharing

  • Employees must not use external email services to share sensitive medical research data, intellectual property, or patient health records.
  • Employees must be wary of receiving emails or opening attachments from unknown senders to protect against phishing scams.

Any employee found violating this policy may face sanctions such as termination or loss of network access privileges depending on the severity of the offense!
